# Battlefield 6 - Frontlines

This is a mod for Battlefield 6 that adds a new game mode called "Frontlines".

## Gameplay

In Frontlines, the map is divided into multiple sectors, each containing capture points. 
The first and last sectors have MCOMs instead of capture points and mimic the base of each team. 
Teams advance through the sectors in a linear fashion, capturing and contesting them to push the frontlines forward. 
Only one sector can be contested at a time.
By capturing all capture points within a sector, a team gains control over that sector and pushes the frontline forward.

### Sector Capture

In the opening stage, teams fight for the central sector of the map.  
Capture rate is a fixed time (no capture multiplier by the amount of players on point), while neutralize rate is increased.

Once captured, another flag becomes available for capture after a 30-second delay. 
The entire play area is open at all times, allowing for wide flanks, vanguard attacks on prospective flags, or booby-trapping of rear flags, but at risk of leaving the currently-contested flag open for easy capture.

### Base Defense

If a team's rear sector is captured, players proceed to play a Rush subsection where MCOMs must be attacked or defended at the team's base. 
The attacking team is given a certain number of tickets (or a certain amount of time) to take down the enemy MCOMs.
Should the attacking team fail to destroy the MCOMs with the tickets allotted, the defenders push back into the flag zone and Conquest-style play continues until either base sector is taken to resume the Rush-style play.

### Win Conditions

Victory is earned upon successful destruction of all the enemy MCOMs. In case of reaching the 40-minute time limit, the
team with more MCOMs still standing wins. If both teams have the same number of MCOMs still standing, the match ends in
a draw if both teams have the same amount of captured sectors and capture points. Otherwise, the team with more captured sectors / capture points wins.

#### Overtime

In case an MCOM is currently being armed while the time limit is reached, an indefinite overtime period begins where the
team that is currently arming the MCOM must complete the arming process to win, while the opposing team must disarm the
MCOM to end the overtime.
